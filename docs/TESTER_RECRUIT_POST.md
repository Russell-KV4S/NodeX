**Looking for Android testers: NodeX, a free AllStarLink client**

I've built NodeX, a free app that connects your phone straight to AllStarLink nodes. You can listen, talk (push-to-talk), keep favourites, link to more than one node, and use it in Android Auto. Google requires at least 12 testers for 14 days before an app can go public on the Play Store, so I could use your help.

To join (takes a minute):

1. Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/testers-nodex
2. Opt in on Google Play: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kv4s.nodex
3. Install NodeX from the Play Store and keep it installed for at least 14 days.

Use the same Google account your phone's Play Store uses. If step 2 says the test isn't available yet, try again in a few days.

Bugs and ideas: https://github.com/Russell-KV4S/NodeX/issues
